I am a passionate outdoorsman and mountaineer, and have been for a very long time. My parents were crucial in instilling that love and curiosity for the outdoors. And thanks to them, I grew up fortunate enough to explore the world from a young age.


I have been hiking for a long time. From before I can even remember actually. But in 2019, I summited Mt. Kilimanjaro and thus began my mountain climbing journey. I have since trekked so many other peaks across many continents. I continue to search for peaks to climb and one day hope to scale the Seven Summits.


Mountain climbing has taught me a lot of lifeskills: problem-solving, cooperation, perseverance, and probably most importantly, leadership. These have all helped me on the slopes, but also in my personal life. I share a little more of my experiences in my TEDx Talk.

summited peaks

Tour du Mont Blanc (2025)

Italy-France-Switzerland 13 Days

The Tour du Mont Blanc is a roughly 170-kilometer (105-mile) trek around Mont Blanc, passing through France, Italy, and Switzerland. The route crosses alpine valleys and high mountain passes, with views of glaciers, peaks, and villages along the way. We completed the circuit in 13 days, following the trail through the Chamonix Valley, Val Ferret, and the Italian and Swiss Alps before returning to France.

Mt. Whitney (2024)

Caifornia, USA 20 Hours

At 14,505 feet (4,421 meters), Mt. Whitney is the highest peak in the contiguous United States and is located in the southern range of the Sierra Nevadas. The Mt. Whitney Trail, climbs through pine forests and alpine terrain before reaching Trail Crest at over 13,000 feet. From there, the trail winds along the western edge of the ridge before reaching the summit.

Half Dome (2024)

Caifornia, USA 14 Hours

Half Dome's distinctive granite face is one of the Yosemite National Park's most recognizable landmarks. We reached the summit via the Mist Trail, crossing Vernal and Nevada Falls before continuing through the Little Yosemite valley and toward the base of the dome. The final ascent follows the famous cables up the steep granite face, with wide views across the Valley from the summit.

Everest Base Camp (2022)

Nepal 14 Days

Everest Base Camp sits at around 5,364 meters (17,598 feet) in Nepal’s Khumbu region. We followed the Dudh Koshi valley through Sherpa villages, Buddhist monasteries, and rugged high-altitude terrain. The route offers us views of Everest and other Himalayan peaks along the way. From Base Camp, the trail continues toward the Khumbu Icefall, where climbers begin their ascent of Mount Everest.

Mt. Kilimanjaro (2019)

Tanzania 7 Days

Mt. Kilimanjaro is the highest mountain in Africa and the world’s highest free-standing mountain is 5,895 meters (19,341 feet) tall. It is located in Tanzania and its summit, Uhuru Peak, sits on the dormant Kibo volcano. The standard routes take several days of trekking through distinct ecological zones before reaching the summit. Kilimanjaro holds a special place as my first major summit.
